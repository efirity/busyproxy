# Privacy & legal URLs for Play

These must stay online **before** and **after** submission.

| Resource | URL | In-app | Web footer |
|----------|-----|--------|------------|
| Privacy Policy | https://busyproxy.net/privacy | Yes (links) | Yes |
| Terms of Service | https://busyproxy.net/terms | Yes | Yes |
| Account deletion instructions | https://busyproxy.net/account-deletion | Yes + in-app delete | Yes |
| Support email | support@busyproxy.net | Yes | Yes |

## Google requirements covered

1. **Privacy policy URL** in Play Console and preferably in-app  
2. **Account deletion**  
   - In-app path (Android Home → Delete account)  
   - Web resource for users who uninstalled the app  
3. **Data safety form** consistent with Privacy Policy  

## Counsel note

Terms and Privacy in this repo are **launch-ready product templates**. Have a
lawyer review before large-scale commercial launch, especially for:

- Residential bandwidth / proxy regulatory risk  
- Carrier ToS interaction  
- GDPR / local privacy (if EU users)  
- Payout / tax wording
